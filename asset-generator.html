File structure

What the ZIP will contain.

Images.xcassets and the flat PNG files below sit together in the same ZIP.

Flat PNGs

├── App_Icon_512x512_{name} 2.png 512×512, bg and logoCTV
├── App_Menu_Closed_256x256_{name} 1.png 256×256, bg and logoCTV
├── App_Menu_Closed_256x256_{name} 2.png 256×256, logo only (transparent)CTV
├── App_Menu_Open_256x144_{name} 2.png 256×144, logo only (transparent)CTV
├── App_Banner_320x180_{name} 1.png 320×180, bg and logoCTV
├── App_Icon_{name}.png 540×540, bg and 16:9 logo on a squareRoku
├── App_Menu_Closed_1080x1080_{name} 1.png 1080×1080, bg and logoRoku
├── App_Menu_Closed_1080x1080_{name} 2.png 1080×1080, logo only (transparent)Roku
├── App_Menu_Open_1920x1080_{name} 1.png 1920×1080, logo only (transparent)Roku
├── App_Logo_Menu_Closed_{name}.png native size, 1:1 logo as uploadedRoku
├── App_Logo_Menu_Open_{name}.png native size, 16:9 logo as uploadedRoku
└── {name}-Splash.png 1920×1080, shared by Roku and CTV
